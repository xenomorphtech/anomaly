Try the workflows

  • Capture — press c (or click the ✎ line at the bottom), type, hit Enter. The thought is inked at the end of today — unfiled, but timestamped.
  • Agent report — click ▶ simulate report (or wait ~8s). A new entry fades in at the bottom; if you've scrolled up, a pill offers the jump.
  • Orient & decide — click the amber ⚑ decision flag at the end of today (or its pin in the mini-map). Replay mode filters the page to that thread; step through it, then commit.

    • Strengths

  • Time is the one structure you never have to maintain — everything has exactly one place.
  • Replay mode turns re-orientation into literal re-reading of the actual history.
  • Cross-project interleaving surfaces rhythms a per-project view hides.

    • Weaknesses

  • Current state ("where is project X now?") must be reconstructed by reading.
  • Busy projects drown quiet ones in the shared stream.
  • Long threads scatter across days — replay helps, but only for flagged decisions.

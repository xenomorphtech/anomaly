Try the workflows

  • Capture — press c or ＋ capture. Type, hit Enter: the thought drops as a drifting card in the empty space near your view. No cluster, no folder — fine.
  • Agent report — click ▶ simulate report (or wait ~8s). The packet-tracer chip glows and a report sticky appears beside the Albion cluster; your camera never moves.
  • Orient & decide — click the amber ⚑ decision card at the edge of Albion. The camera flies in and the evidence trail fans out, card by card, leading to the decision. Commit there.
  • Drag to pan · scroll to zoom · click the sector map to jump.

    • Strengths

  • Spatial memory is free indexing — you remember where things live.
  • Unfiled captures are first-class citizens of the map, not a guilt-inducing inbox.
  • The evidence trail makes decision context literally lead somewhere.

    • Weaknesses

  • No inherent ordering — recency and urgency don't jump out without extra signals.
  • Manual layout becomes a chore as clusters grow and collide.
  • Poor fit for dense text scanning; lots of camera motion.

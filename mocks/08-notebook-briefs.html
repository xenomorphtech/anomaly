Try the workflows

  1. Capture — press c or the ✎ Capture button, type a thought, hit Enter. It lands in the Inbox scratchpad doc (left sidebar) with no filing required.
  2. Agent report — click ▶ simulate agent report (or wait ~8s). A green diff-styled entry appends to the Albion brief's Log, and its “State of the world” ripples to show it was rewritten.
  3. Orient & decide — open the Albion Automation brief and click the amber ⚑ decision callout. It expands into a decision memo; read the trail, then commit one of the two options.

Paradigm trade-offs

Strengths

  • Re-reading a maintained summary is the fastest way to reload context after days away.
  • Agent output lands as prose in the same place you think — no separate ops console.
  • Decisions live inside their narrative, so the “why” never detaches from the “what”.

Weaknesses

  • Summaries rot unless something (you or an agent) actually rewrites them.
  • Poor at cross-project glanceability — one doc visible at a time.
  • Long logs get heavy; needs periodic gardening/compaction.

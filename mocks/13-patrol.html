Try the workflows

  1. Capture — press c (or the + capture button). Type a thought, Enter. It lands unclassified in the Queue stop — no filing required.
  2. Alert contract — click ▶ simulate report (also auto-fires once ~8s in). Watch: ping on the ring, corner toast, badge on the Albion dial — your view never moves. Space jumps to it, Esc brings the camera back.
  3. The sweep — press 1 2 3 4 (or ride Tab/. around the loop). Every stop leads with its delta since you left; staleness dials refill as fog clears.
  4. Idle button — click ⚠ idle/blocked 2 (or i) and click through both stuck agents: why they're stuck + suggested next action.
  5. Deep recovery + decision — visit Paper (3) or Apartment (4) and hit Rebuild context for the replay. Then open the gate-desync decision (Albion card or Queue stop), replay its history, commit — navigator unblocks, idle count drops.

Patrol — honest trade-offs

Strengths

  • The 1-2-3-4 sweep is a rehearsed ~10s ritual; delta-first cards make re-entry nearly free.
  • Triage happens on the context it belongs to — no separate inbox to reconcile.
  • Staleness dials turn "when did I last look?" into a glance.

Weaknesses

  • One full-screen card at a time — no cross-project side-by-side.
  • A linear ring buries urgent stops mid-cycle; Space mitigates but breaks the rhythm.
  • Skip patrol for a week and every stop goes cold — replay recovery gets expensive.

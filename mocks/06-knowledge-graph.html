Try the workflows

  1. Capture — press c or hit ＋ capture. Your thought drops in as an untethered node orbiting the center. No filing required.
  2. Agent report — click ▶ simulate agent report (or wait ~8s). Watch the packet-tracer node ping in the Albion cluster.
  3. Orient & decide — click the glowing amber ◆ Gate desync fix diamond (Albion cluster, upper-left). Its history fans out clockwise; read the sequence in the side panel, then commit.

Strengths

  • Relationships are first-class: you can see navigator chained to the decision blocking it.
  • Spatial memory — clusters stay put, so you re-orient by location, not by search.
  • Ambient status: pulse/ring animations show the whole fleet's state at a glance.

Weaknesses

  • Poor for dense text — long reports still need a side panel to be readable.
  • Doesn't scale past ~100 nodes without clustering/filtering.
  • Captures still accumulate as orbiters; the graph doesn't triage them for you.

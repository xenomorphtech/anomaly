Try the workflows

  1. Capture — press c or the ✎ capture button, type, Enter. It drops into the dashed ✎ inbox chip on the shelf — no card, no filing.
  2. Agent report — click ▶ simulate agent report (or wait ~8s). Watch the Albion card lower in the stack: its "changed since" badge bumps and glows. Background work accumulates onto the resume card.
  3. Orient & decide — click the Albion card to bring it to top (it auto-expands), then click the red ⚑ decision chip. The briefing replays step-by-step; commit at the end.

Paradigm trade-offs

Strengths

  • Matches how attention actually works: one focus, everything else parked with a resume note.
  • "What changed since" makes background agent work legible at a glance.
  • Push/pop is an honest cost signal — a deep stack is visible overload.

Weaknesses

  • Only one context fully visible; poor for cross-project comparison.
  • Stack order is a lie if you actually multitask in minutes, not hours.
  • Resume notes need discipline (or an agent) to stay truthful.

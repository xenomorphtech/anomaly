Try the workflows

  1. Capture — press c or the ＋ Capture button, type anything. It lands unread in Unsorted at the top of the inbox, unclassified.
  2. Agent report — click ▶ simulate agent report in the top bar (or wait ~8s). A new unread item from packet-tracer arrives; your reading pane is untouched.
  3. Orient & decide — open the “Gate desync fix” item (⚑ decision, in Albion / Inbox). The reading pane stitches the full history into one brief; commit at the bottom.
Bonus: triage items with Assign / Snooze / Done and ride the inbox down to zero.

Strengths

  • One familiar triage muscle — nothing (report, thought, decision) can slip past you unseen.
  • Unread counts show exactly where attention is owed; inbox zero is a real, reachable “done”.
  • Reading pane forces one-thing-at-a-time focus; the decision brief reads like a letter.

Weaknesses

  • Flattens all projects into one stream — no spatial or structural memory.
  • Rewards clearing items over actually re-orienting; “done” ≠ understood.
  • Cross-project overview requires clicking contexts one by one.

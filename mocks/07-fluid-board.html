Try the workflows

  1. Capture — press c or hit the ＋ Capture button (bottom-right). Your thought lands as a loose card in the top gutter — no project required. Drag it into a lane to assign it later.
  2. Agent report — click ▶ simulate agent report up top (auto-fires once ~8s after load). Watch the packet-tracer chip flash on the “Fix gate desync” card.
  3. Orient & decide — click the red Fix gate desync card in the Albion lane. It expands into the full decision history; commit a fix to unblock navigator.
Also: drag any card. Its state badge follows the todo→doing→done gradient; dropping in another lane reassigns it.

ⓘ Honest comparison

Strengths
  • Spatial memory: you re-find things by where they drifted, not by label.
  • Capture is truly frictionless — gutters make “unsorted” a first-class place.
  • Progress is visible at a glance as rightward drift per lane.
Weaknesses
  • Free positions rot: an untended board becomes a junk drawer.
  • No timeline — event order lives only inside card histories.
  • Scales poorly past ~6 projects / dozens of cards.

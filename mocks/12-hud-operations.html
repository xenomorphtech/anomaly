TRY THE WORKFLOWS
  1. Capture — press C (or + CAPTURE), type, Enter. It lands in INBOX instantly, unclassified, and rolls across the ticker.
  2. Alert contract — click ▶ SIM REPORT (or wait ~8s). Watch: minimap ping + corner toast + rail badge only — the center view never moves. Then Space jumps to it, Esc brings the camera back.
  3. The sweep — press 1 2 3 4 in sequence. Each stop leads with a delta-first resume header; staleness fog burns off as you visit.
  4. Idle button — click the amber IDLE/BLOCKED button (bottom right, or I) twice to cycle both stalled agents, each with a suggested next action.
  5. Deep recovery + decision — press 3 or 4 (cold/frozen) and take the DEEP RESYNC briefing; open the gate-desync decision from Albion (or the amber ◆ on the minimap), re-read the context trail, commit a fix — navigator unblocks and the idle count drops.
ⓘ HONEST COMPARE

STRENGTHS

+ Fixed chrome builds muscle memory: every control lives in the same pixels forever — glances cost nothing.

+ Each project gets its best-fit lens (brief / thread / board) without paying any navigation cost; the HUD remembers your choice.

+ Periphery stays alive during deep focus: ticker, minimap, fleet and idle count update without ever stealing the center.

WEAKNESSES

− The stable frame permanently spends ~25% of the screen on periphery, even when you want pure focus.

− Three view modes per project is real cognitive surface — you must remember which lens you left each project in.

− A busy fleet makes the ticker noisy; it rotates, so a glance can miss the one line that mattered.

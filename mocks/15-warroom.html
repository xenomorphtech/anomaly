Try the workflows

  1. Capture — press c (or click the input, top-left), type a thought, Enter. It lands at the top of the triage queue, unclassified — file it later with o.
  2. Alert contract — click ▶ simulate report (or wait ~8s). Watch: ping on the minimap, corner toast, unseen badge. Your view never moves. Space jumps to the alert; Esc brings the camera back.
  3. The sweep — press 1 2 3 4 in sequence. Each stop leads with a delta-first resume header; fog clears as you visit each project.
  4. Idle button — click the amber idle/blocked button (right pane, or i) repeatedly to cycle through stalled agents with reasons and suggested actions.
  5. Deep recovery + decision — press 3 or 4 and take the offered deep recovery briefing. Then open the gate-desync decision from the triage queue, re-read the accumulated context, and commit — navigator unblocks in the production bar.

Strengths / weaknesses

Strengths

  • Every interrupt lands in one triageable queue — nothing modal, nothing lost, keyboard-speed processing.
  • The center brief is a maintained "state of the world", so re-orientation is reading, not archaeology.
  • Whole-fleet capacity and stalls are visible at all times in the production bar.

Weaknesses

  • Three dense panes demand screen width and sustained attention — hostile on a laptop in a meeting.
  • Briefs stay truthful only if curated; a stale brief is worse than no brief.
  • The triage queue can itself become a guilt inbox if arrivals outpace processing.

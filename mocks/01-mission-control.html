Try the workflows

  • Capture — press c or the + Capture button, type, hit Enter. It lands in Unsorted intake and the event log — no filing.
  • Agent report — click ▶ Simulate report (or wait ~8s). Watch the fleet tile flash, the log, and the toast.
  • Orient & decide — click the red Gate desync alert (top strip) or the pending decision in the Albion lane. Ack the briefing, then commit a choice.

    • Strengths

  • Everything urgent is visible in one glance — alert strip makes pending decisions impossible to miss.
  • Fleet board gives instant cross-project agent status without digging.
  • Briefing-ack ritual forces genuine re-reading before committing.

    • Weaknesses

  • Density is hostile to calm browsing; everything shouts at once.
  • Weak sense of history/narrative — the log is a ticker, not a story.
  • Scales poorly past ~8 projects without lane triage.
